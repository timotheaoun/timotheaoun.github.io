Northwest Territories

Imagine vast skies, deep lakes, and welcoming communities.

Located in the heart of Northern Canada, the Northwest Territories offer a rare blend of wilderness, vibrant Indigenous culture, and career opportunities suited for young people.



Landscapes & must-see places

Nahanni National Park

Explore one of the most breathtaking UNESCO World Heritage Sites in Canada. Nahanni offers pure wilderness, majestic waterfalls and the spirit of the North at its best.

Great Slave Lake

And Great Bear Lake

Two iconic lakes: vast bodies of water, fishing, boating, bright summers, and frozen winters.

Tuktoyaktuk & Inuvik Region

A region rich in indigenous culture, with unique Arctic landscapes, ice roads, and a glimpse into life in the Far North.

Old Town & Prince of Wales Northern Heritage Centre(Yellowknife)

The historic district of the capital with its old miners' cabins, local shops, and art galleries. Iconic for urban walks in the North and for experiencing the unique atmosphere of Yellowknife. The village is home to the main museum of the Northwest Territories.

Climate & seasons

The seasons are marked: long days in summer, dry and cold winters favorable to auroras and winter activities. Life is organized around the seasons.

Why would a young immigrant choose the Northwest Territories?

Firstly, for the proximity to nature and the possibility of an active lifestyle. Secondly, for a welcoming community where one quickly makes friends. Next, there are opportunities in the public sector, tourism, and increasingly in digital or creative activities. Finally, the cultural uniqueness, the strong presence of Indigenous peoples, makes the Territories a place of personal enrichment.
